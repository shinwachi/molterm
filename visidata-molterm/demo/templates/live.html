A real VisiData on the server with 1000 made-up amides (40 acids × 25 amines), each drawn beside its SMILES with its formula, MW, cLogP, TPSA, H-bond donors and acceptors, rotatable bonds, QED and alerts. Click the screen to use it. Alt+g or Chart plots cLogP against MW below the table, a dot for each compound coloured by its series, with a card showing the molecule under the cursor: Alt+n and Alt+b step from dot to dot and the table's cursor follows, Tab goes to the table, where moving the cursor moves the chart's to that row's dot, Enter on the chart opens the compounds under its cursor, and q closes the chart. On the sheet, Alt+o opens a molecule, Alt+Shift+E or Edit molecule draws it in molterm's editor (Ctrl+Q puts it back, Ctrl+C cancels, U undoes), Alt+t switches to Braille. Move with the arrows or h j k l, sort with [ and ], search with /, select with s or |, count with Shift+F, plot with ., and q to go back. This demo keeps to the sheet in memory: there is no Python, shell or file access, and the session ends after 10 minutes without a key, or 30 in all.
